Before the search starts

Jobsy needs your direction, not only your document.

A CV tells part of the story. Jobsy also needs to know what you can do and what you actually want now.

1

Who you are

Your background, location, languages and the practical situation around your search.

2

What you have done

Your experience, skills, past roles and the kind of work you can credibly do.

3

What you could do

Several possible professions or role families, if your experience can point in more than one direction.

4

What you want now

The role, country, schedule or next step you want Jobsy to search for first.

Your CV is not the search query.

Someone may have worked as a cook and a warehouse worker, but now want logistics work in Sweden. That is the target Jobsy should search for.

What Jobsy does
