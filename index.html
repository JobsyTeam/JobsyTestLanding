JBW - Jobsy Waves

JBW show how your contribution spreads.

JBW are not money. They do not have a fixed EUR value and cannot be exchanged at a fixed rate.

They are simply Jobsy's way of beginning to measure how a useful share or contribution moves through the community.

Money stays secondary: if someone joins from your share and becomes a paid user, Jobsy can give you €1 as a simple thank-you.
