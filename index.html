Jobsy Active

Jobsy Active automates the most time-consuming parts of job searching. Instead of manually sending applications every day, Jobsy can handle the process for you.

CV translation for employers

Jobsy automatically translates your CV into the employer's language, making it easier to apply to companies in different countries without rewriting your resume.

AI-generated cover letters

Jobsy creates a unique cover letter for every company. Each message keeps the same meaning but is slightly different to avoid spam filters and increase the chances of a response.

Applications sent from your own email

Jobsy connects to your email using an app password and sends applications through your mailbox.

  • Replies go directly to you.
  • Jobsy cannot access your conversations.
  • Your communication remains private.
  • The system is fully GDPR-friendly.

Automatic job applications

Jobsy can automatically send 1000–1200 job applications per month while respecting safe sending limits. This allows you to reach far more companies than traditional job searching.
