The story of Jobsy

It started with finding work.

Then we saw that work is only one part of people's real lives.

People ask about changing profession, education, language, documents, housing and everyday life in another country. Very often, someone else has already lived through the same problem.

Jobsy Waves

What if useful experience could also help the person who shared it?

Jobsy Waves is an experiment.

We want to learn how useful participation in Jobsy can eventually be measured and monetized fairly for users.

What creates value?
QuestionsWhat do people need to know?

Pilot

We are building this mechanism carefully.

The first Waves and reward mechanisms are already being tested. The detailed rules will live separately on a dedicated Jobsy Waves page.

How Jobsy Waves works

The larger goal is simple: if people's contribution makes Jobsy more useful, we want to explore a fair way for some of that value to return to the people who helped create it.
